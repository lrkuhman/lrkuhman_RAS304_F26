    Bill of Materials

    Overview

    Everything needed to build my LiDAR subsystem board. Reference designators match the schematic. Prices are in US dollars without shipping. Parts come from DigiKey except the LiDAR, the fuse holder, and the 0805 component book, which supplies every resistor, ceramic capacitor, and LED. Datasheets for book parts are for equivalent manufacturer parts.
